Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 November 2026

1. About KamHub

KamHub is an Android workspace for school management, property rent management, booking slips, business and cash books, and document editing.

The app can store information entered by users, including names, contact details, student records, tenant information, booking details, financial transactions, and documents.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

KamHub stores the records and files you create or import on your device.

This information may include personal and financial details about other people. Users are responsible for obtaining appropriate permission to record and share this information.

3. Local Network Features

Some School Management features support device-to-device transfers over a local Wi-Fi network.

When these features are used, selected school information may be transferred to another device on that network.

KamHub requests Internet and Wi-Fi state permissions to support networking features. These permissions do not by themselves mean that information is uploaded to a KamHub cloud server.

4. Exports, Sharing, and Backups

Users may choose to export, print, share, back up, restore, or transfer information.

When information is shared or exported, the selected files or records may be handled by another app, storage provider, or device.

Those destinations may have their own privacy practices. Users should keep backups and exported documents secure.

5. Data Use and Disclosure

KamHub uses entered information to provide management, calculations, reporting, document creation, and transfer features requested by users.

Information may leave the device when users initiate sharing, exporting, backup, or local-network transfers.

No advertising or analytics service was identified in the reviewed app source. The final published version and any added third-party components must be checked for changes to data practices.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Records remain in the app until users edit or delete them through available features, clear app data, or uninstall the application.

Previously exported, shared, or backed-up copies are not automatically deleted when a record is removed from the app.

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information. Users should preserve a backup beforehand if necessary.

7. Data Security

KamHub uses device storage and includes backup protection features.

No software can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices, backups, credentials, and shared files.

8. Children's Information

School administrators may enter student records, including information about minors, for school management purposes.

Authorized users should have an appropriate basis to process these records and should not share student information without authorization.

KamHub is a management application and is not marketed directly to children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KamHub's features or data practices change.

Updated information will be made available through this webpage and, where applicable, within the application.
